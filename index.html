The terms, plainly

What the app will and will not do with what you write.

Each of these is enforced in the schema and on the server, not promised by a switch on a settings screen that the client could forget to send.

Private by default
An entry marked private is never sent for interpretation. The exclusion is enforced on the server, not by a switch the client can forget.
Twenty a day
Every account gets twenty interpretations per UTC day, counted on the server. Reopening a result you already have costs nothing.
Which version you get
The rewrite described here is 1.2.0, and it is in TestFlight by invitation. The App Store serves 1.0.6, which predates it. There is no public beta link yet.
What it costs
Free to download, and no ads anywhere in the app. A paid membership is not on sale yet; when it is, logging and your archive stay free.
Your records only
The Pattern Researcher reads your entries and nothing else. It names its own uncertainty and surfaces the cases that argue against it.
Sharing is a copy
Posting to a Collective creates a separate, redacted record that you confirm first. It never widens access to the entry it came from.
Delete means delete
Correct a reading, remove what was derived from it, or close the account and have the whole record go with it.
Not therapy
No diagnosis, no treatment advice, no prophecy, and no claim that a named person produced the output.
An account, always
Google, email, or Sign in with Apple before anything else. There is no guest mode, because there is nowhere for a guest's entries to survive.
